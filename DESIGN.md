# Floral invitation design

White stationery, floral pink, watercolor garden roses and peonies, with sage botanical details. Existing wedding information, RSVP, calendar downloads, map link, and English/Thai translations are retained.

## Motion

- Click the seal or Open invitation to break the seal, lift the flap, and slide out the card.
- The hero names, invitation text, botanical drawing, and date enter in sequence.
- Petals drift and flowers gently sway throughout the invitation.
- Calendar, personal note, schedule cards, venue, countdown, RSVP, and footer reveal on scroll.
- Illustrated schedule icons draw into view; changing countdown numbers animate while visible.
- Pause motion is always available. System reduced-motion preferences are respected by default; guests can explicitly resume motion.
- Escape opens the invitation immediately. Focus remains in the opening dialog until opened, then moves to the couple's names. Direct section links bypass the envelope.
- Without JavaScript the invitation is readable immediately. There is no automatic music.

## Artwork

Project asset: `assets/wedding-florals.webp` (1000 × 667, transparent WebP, approximately 185 KiB).

Created using the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and encoded as WebP while retaining transparency. The generated source PNG is retained in the local generated-images directory.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative floral artwork for a luxury wedding invitation website. Create a fine botanical watercolor painting of one loose, asymmetrical spray of blush pink garden roses, pale pink peonies, small white cosmos and delicate sage green leaves. Natural elegant curved stems and airy small buds. Vintage fine art watercolor with exquisite layered translucent petals and subtle pencil detail, romantic and luminous, restrained pale pink and white palette with dusty sage. Composition: a single diagonal floral spray from lower left to upper right, blooms clustered at the lower left, tapering into airy stems at upper right, all flowers fully contained with a generous transparent margin. Genuinely transparent background with alpha, no paper rectangle, no vase, no text, no letters, no watermark. This will be layered over white stationery and gently animated. Landscape 1536x1024.
